**What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?**
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science focused on building smart machines capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence—such as reasoning, learning from experience, recognizing patterns, understanding natural language, and making decisions.

**Key Layers of AI**
The diagram above illustrates how the core concepts fit together as nested subsets:

**1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)**
- **The Big Picture:** Any system or program that simulates human cognitive functions to solve problems or execute tasks autonomously.
- _Examples:_ Chess-playing algorithms, voice assistants (Siri, Alexa), recommendation engines.

**2. Machine Learning (ML)** _(Subset of AI)_
- **Learning from Data:** Instead of hard-coding exact rules, algorithms analyze data to discover patterns and improve their performance over time automatically.
- _Examples:_ Spam filters, fraud detection, predictive text.

**3. Deep Learning (DL)** _(Subset of Machine Learning)_
 - **Brain-Inspired Networks:** Uses artificial neural networks with many layers (hence "deep") to process vast, unstructured data like images, audio, and raw text.
- _Examples:_ Large Language Models (LLMs), self-driving car vision systems, facial recognition.
